The TMTAS Exchange event is found automatically from its title ("SpeedTAS Season2 #30", "SpeedTAS TM20 #5"). If it can't be found you can type the event ID in the field next to the edition. For times, an optional Players-tab column with "TMX", "Exchange" or "Alias" in the header can hold a player's exchange username when it's very different from their sheet name.
